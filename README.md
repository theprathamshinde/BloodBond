Problem Statement:
To develop a application for seamless communication between blood donors and
hospitals, ensuring timely blood donations and distribution to patients who needs blood in emergency.

Objectives:
Ensure timely access and sufficient blood supplies for medical emergencies and transfusion needs.

Indicator 3.8.1: Universal health coverage implies that all people receive the quality health services they need.
